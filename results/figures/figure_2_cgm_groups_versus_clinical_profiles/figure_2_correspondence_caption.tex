\caption{Correspondence between clinical profiles and CGM-defined groups in type~2 diabetes treated without insulin ($N=458$; full CGM record). CGM groups G1--G3 come from $k$-means ($k=3$) on extended CGM features; row labels give each group's size and median participant mean glucose. (A)~Clinical profile composition of each CGM group: cells show counts and, in parentheses, percentages within the CGM group; each column is shaded in its profile colour from white (0\%) to full colour (60\%); agreement between the two partitions is given as the adjusted Rand index (ARI) with 95\% bootstrap CI. (B)~Kruskal--Wallis rank-based effect sizes ($\eta^2$, 95\% bootstrap CI) for clinical variables across CGM groups; HbA1c is shown on a separate scale. (C)~Cross-validated macro AUROC for predicting C1--C3 from HbA1c alone, CGM features alone, and HbA1c plus CGM; bars are 95\% bootstrap CIs, the bracket gives the paired bootstrap gain of HbA1c plus CGM over HbA1c alone, and no interval was stored for the combined model itself. The dotted line marks chance (0.50). (D)~Cross-validated $R^2$ (95\% CI) for predicting each clinical variable from CGM features; the vertical line marks $R^2=0$.}
