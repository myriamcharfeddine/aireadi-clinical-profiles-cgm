# Decision log

Dated decisions that shape the analyses. The full pre-registered designs are in `preregistration/`. Decisions made before any outcome was seen are marked *pre-registered*.

## Profile derivation (2026-08-03, frozen)

- Six factors: age, BMI, HbA1c, C-peptide, TG/HDL (mg/dL over mg/dL), waist-to-hip ratio. Laboratory values dated after the end of CGM are set to missing.
- Fitting is per stratum, on training participants only. The steps are:
  - iterative imputation (complete cases for healthy);
  - log(1 + x) where |skew| > 1;
  - standardization;
  - k-means with 100 initializations and seed 42.
- k = 3 for both diabetes strata and k = 2 for healthy and prediabetes, chosen by silhouette, gap statistic and bootstrap stability. The summary is in `results/profile_derivation/k_selection_frozen.json`. It is not rerun here (decision D3 below).

## Reverse direction (2026-10-14, pre-registered)

1. The primary partition uses the extended features over the full record. Extended 48 h, minimal full record and minimal 48 h are secondary.
2. B5 variant (a), the incremental value of CGM over HbA1c, is run. Variant (b) is not.
3. Coverage floor: 70% of 576 readings in the first 48 h, and 1,000 valid readings over the full record.
4. Permutations: 200 with fixed hyperparameters. Any primary-partition p value between 0.01 and 0.10 is rerun with 1,000.

## Other glycemic strata (2026-10-15, pre-registered)

1. A criterion stated through a CI requires both the unadjusted 95% CI and a Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 in the same family.
2. Specificity readings: supported, not specific, or inconclusive.
3. All 564 healthy participants are assigned through the T2D pipeline, including the 26 without a profile of their own.
4. In S2, the full record is primary. The 48 h window is a secondary partition at the same k and enters no reading.

## Temporal reproducibility (2026-10-15 and 2026-10-16, pre-registered before outcomes)

1. A held-out ARI ≥ 0.50 with kappa < 0.60 is read as moderate.
2. The window A pipeline is fit on training participants eligible in both windows.
3. The C2 contrast rule uses no Holm correction (an intersection-union test). Unadjusted p values are reported.
4. Days 1 to 3 versus days 4 to 6 is primary, chosen on eligibility and weekend balance before any outcome was seen. Days 1 to 4 versus days 5 to 8 is a sensitivity design.
5. The held-out n is masked (fewer than 5 held-out participants are excluded). Every statistic computed on a masked n is rounded before it is tracked.
6. Healthy and prediabetes are not analysed over time.

## Rebuild of this repository (2026-10-19)

**D1. Figures.**
- The canonical figures are the three in the manuscript, drawn from the same data and layout as `plot_phase0_raw_t2d_main_figure.py`, `plot_reverse_figure.py` and `plot_strata_figure.py`.
- Fig S1 is the temporal v2 three-panel design.
- The print redraws (`fig1_forward_cgm_by_profile`, `fig2_correspondence`, `figS_reverse_supplement`, `fig3_strata_summary`, `plot_fig2_paper`) stay in the record repository only.

**D2. Tables.**
- Table 1 uses the manuscript's format:
  - medians rounded half up (age to integers; BMI, HbA1c, C-peptide and TG/HDL to 1 decimal; waist-to-hip to 2);
  - IQR written with an en dash;
  - P to 3 decimals, with a floor of <0.001;
  - race labels "White" and "Other or not reported".
- Supplementary Tables S1 (full Table 1) and S2 (other strata) use the same display rules.
- Their cell masks, counts and P values are identical to the record's tables. Rendered in the record's own format, they match the record cell for cell (`docs/VERIFICATION.md`).
- Tables 2 and 3 are now generated by `analyses/06_figures_and_tables.py`. In the record they were typed into the manuscript by hand.
- Half-up rounding is applied once, to the unrounded value. The manuscript's Table 1 had been rounded twice by hand, once from the 2-decimal record table. That changes one cell, the C1 C-peptide lower quartile: 1.6475 gives 1.6, not 1.7 (`MANUSCRIPT_FIXES.md`).

**D3. Profile k-selection.** It is not rerun; its summary is a frozen input. `analyses/01` refits the chosen k in all four strata and stops unless the centroids equal the frozen ones exactly. `tests/test_profiles_refit.py` checks the same thing with 0 label differences. The joblib pipelines and assignment files of the record are therefore not needed.

**D4. Analyses kept.** Only what the manuscript names was ported:
- forward Kruskal-Wallis with η² bootstrap CIs, pairwise differences, Dunn-Holm and ANOVA;
- the training-only, held-out, margin > 0.20 and complete-case subsets;
- split dependence and the GLP-1/GIP-stratified contrasts;
- the reverse partitions, agreement, prediction and two-group CGM clustering;
- the strata S1, S2 and S3 analyses and the pooled B6;
- the temporal days 1 to 3 versus days 4 to 6 design, plus days 4 to 6's 4-day counterpart for the open markers of Fig S1B.

These were dropped:
- the k-means stability refits;
- the coverage subset;
- the rank-inverse-normal tests;
- the rank regression;
- the medication-by-cluster tables;
- the GMM and BIC scans;
- the refit bootstrap of agreement;
- the B6 clinical η²;
- the strata pairwise differences and strata split dependence;
- the split composition check;
- the strata k = 3 sensitivity;
- the four other temporal designs, the temporal T0 representativeness and weekday tables, the window-B refit and the GLP-1 temporal split.

Each of these used its own random stream, so dropping it changes no kept draw.

Three groups were kept even though the manuscript does not quote all their rows, because they share a seeded stream with quoted values:
- all five reverse partitions in B3 and B4 (the "3 of 15" sentence also needs them);
- the full B5 grid, whose permutation seeds are the base seed plus the running row count;
- all four S3 assignment variants, whose draws interleave with the quoted prediabetes interval.
